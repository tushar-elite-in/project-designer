# Primary source map

Use these as starting points for authoritative research, not as a fixed stack or universal checklist. Open the exact relevant page, verify current versions/status, and cite sources in user-facing plans when claims depend on current facts. Prefer the standards body, platform owner, vendor documentation, or maintainer over secondary summaries.

## Domain-specific / regulated systems
Find the authoritative regulator, standards body, platform owner, or vendor for the actual jurisdiction and domain (for example healthcare, payments, education, public sector, transport, industrial control, or telecom). Verify applicability and current edition. These examples do not imply a project is subject to a specific law or standard.
